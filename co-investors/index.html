Investment Focus

What we invest in

We back early-stage technology companies with global potential. We bet on strong teams and defensible technology — evaluated by people who genuinely understand the field.

HealthTech & MedTech

Digital health, diagnostics, AI in medicine and medical devices. One of our strongest verticals, with a dedicated in-house expert group.

Cybersecurity

Security technology and software. We have people behind us who have built and exited world-class companies in the field.

AI, Automation & Robotics

Artificial intelligence, process automation and robotics across industries. We look for teams turning cutting-edge AI into real-world products and measurable efficiency gains.

SaaS & E-commerce

Software products with recurring revenue and online businesses with fast scalability and clear unit economics.

Deep Tech & High Tech

Breakthrough technologies — from quantum computing and space tech to advanced AI. We look for defensible innovation.

University spin-offs

We actively work with universities and help bring research to market. A unique source of high-quality deal flow.

CEE & Europe

Geographically we focus on Czechia and the wider CEE region, backing companies built to scale globally. We are especially strong in and around Brno.

Why us

Why co-invest with Garage Angels

We are a group of entrepreneurs who have built — or are still building — their own companies, some with exits in the hundreds of millions to billions of CZK. We invest our own money and open our deal flow to partners.

Shared deal flow

We open our vetted deals to other angel investors and VC funds. We pool capital, share risk and expand the network of contacts for our portfolio companies.

Sector expert groups

We run in-house expert groups: HealthTech & MedTech, Cybersecurity, E-commerce, SaaS and Deep Tech. Every project is assessed by people who understand the field.

Own capital, no artificial pressure

We invest exclusively our own money. No LPs, no artificial pressure for a quick exit. We decide based on what makes sense for the company.

Hands-on support

We advise, mentor and open doors. The company stays with the founders — we bring experience, contacts and a sector perspective.

Partnership with JIC

We partner with the South Moravian Innovation Centre (JIC) — one of the strongest startup hubs in the region.

Clean, transparent structure

Every deal sits in its own dedicated SPV with a standard shareholders' agreement. Clear ownership, clear rules and professional administration for all investors.
